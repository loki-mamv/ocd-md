Organizations, directories, and reading worth your time. Everything here comes from a government health agency or an established OCD organization.

⚠️ If you are in crisis

• Thoughts of suicide or of harming yourself
• Feeling unable to keep yourself safe
• A mental health emergency of any kind

Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) — free and available 24/7. Call 911 in an immediate emergency.
